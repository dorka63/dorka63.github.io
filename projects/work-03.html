Three Bodies — One Identity

Artist
Anastasiia Murzina
Date
2026
Location
Dubna
Medium
Interactive installation

About the work

The work invites viewers to become part of an intricate dance of three bodies and to see the fundamental laws of nature from a different perspective. In this interactive installation, a person’s identity becomes the basis for a unique trajectory of three objects bound by gravity: each viewer gives rise to a distinct form of movement and rhythm.

The beauty of mathematics and physics is often hidden behind a multitude of equations. Yet even in the exact sciences, researchers do not always have all the answers, and the three-body problem is one such example. It has no universal solution, and people continue to investigate the chaos generated by three massive objects and the gravitational interaction that binds them.

Anastasiia Murzina invites viewers to discover how their own presence connects with a remarkably beautiful aspect of our Universe. Who knows — perhaps somewhere in its infinite expanses, there are stars or planets moving along trajectories that correspond uniquely to you…
