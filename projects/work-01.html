About the work

[You stand in front of a mirror. However, instead of your reflection, you see only empty space behind you. Authors consider death as a physical absence and suggest a viewer to imagine a reality without them. Erasing the reflection, this artwork asks about a boundary of disappearance: what departs along with the body, and what remains beyond the visible realm? «I don't exist" does not mean «I have never existed». Disappearance does not negate the life that has been lived and changes pweson left after them. Their presence lives on in the memories, stories, and experiences of other people. ]
