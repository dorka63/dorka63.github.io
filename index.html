Topics

My interests lie at the intersection of scientific inquiry and lived experience: how we perceive the world, relate to others, and make sense of our presence within systems we cannot fully understand or control.

01 Science & the human perspective
The beauty of physical laws and mathematical models; the possibilities and limits of knowledge; and the place of the human observer within scientific and academic worlds.
02 Perception & constructed realities
Sensory experience, subjective realities, and the ways different channels of perception shape one another. The distance between what is present, what is perceived, and what is understood.
03 Identity & vulnerability
The self in relation to the body, mental states, illness, and dependency. Experiences of alienation and the boundaries between normality, difference, and social acceptance.
04 Connection & belonging
Intimacy, loneliness, and the difficulty of reaching another person. Shared and conflicting frames of reference, intersecting lives, social roles, separation, and missed possibilities.
05 Memory & mediated culture
Nostalgia, personal and collective memory, and the textures of analogue and digital life. Internet cultures, subcultures, artificial intelligence, and relationships shaped by media.
06 Mortality, uncertainty & traces
Death, absence, fear, and the limits of control. Catastrophe and its aftermath; the search for meaning; and what remains of a person in the lives of others.
